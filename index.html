Hexagon Map Tiles

Turn any address or lat/lon into a clipped, hexagonal 3D city tile — buildings, roads, water, greenery and trees pulled live from OpenStreetMap, previewed in the browser and exportable as a GLB. Everything runs client-side; no backend.
